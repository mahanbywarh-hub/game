LORE CODEX

PROLOGUE — THE ANGEL WHO CHOSE TO FALL

Before the sky had a name, the First Halo became a bridge between the living world and the sea of memories. The angels swore that no memory would ever be sacrificed. After the First War, however, the Celestial Council began erasing painful memories. Each stolen memory became light that kept the Halo alive. The heavens shone with the price of millions of forgotten lives.

Sera, guardian of the Lower Gate, was ordered to seal it. Children were trapped behind its doors. She defied the order and shattered her own Halo to free them. Her wings were stained not by evil, but by ash and the blood paid to break the lock. The Council erased her name and entrusted five seals to five guardians.

EIREN — THE VOICE WITHIN THE HALO

Eiren was the last scribe of the Book of Names. Before the archive fell, he hid his voice inside a crack in Sera’s Halo. He is not a living human, but a memory that refuses to end. Each broken seal restores part of Sera’s past, while Eiren’s voice grows weaker. In the end, saving the world may mean saying goodbye to the only voice that stayed beside her.

THE ENDINGS

“A Sky Without Chains” breaks the Halo and frees every name, forcing the world to rebuild without borrowed light. “The Heavy Light” restores the Halo and brings safety back, but the names are imprisoned once more. Neither ending is without a cost.
